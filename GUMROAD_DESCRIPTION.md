# Gumroad Product Description — Hitoku Draft

## Full Description

**Hitoku Draft** — voice-powered text editing for macOS.

Select text in any app, press a hotkey, and speak your edit. Hitoku rewrites it in place — fix grammar, rephrase, translate, or draft from scratch. Everything runs locally on Apple Silicon. No cloud. No subscription. No data leaves your Mac.

**How it works:**
- Select text anywhere, press the hotkey, speak your instruction
- Grammar fix with one shortcut — auto-detects English, Italian, French, German
- No text selected? Just speak — Hitoku drafts new content at your cursor

**What you get:**
- Native macOS menu bar app (Apple Silicon)
- On-device LLM and speech-to-text — fully offline
- Works in any app: Mail, Notes, Slack, VS Code, browsers, everywhere
- One-time purchase, free updates
